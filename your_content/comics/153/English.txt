1. Maki (Tabaxi girl) lies down on the couch with her head in Tamilda's lap, allowing (or rather, expecting) Tamilda to give her scritches. This level of intimacy in a person she just met is confusing for Tamilda, but given that Maki is a Tabaxi (cat-person), it's not that weird for her.

Maki: "And my parents were invited because they own the theatre, so of COURSE they made me come. Because seeing a rich guy marry an actress will somehow give me "Culture". You know what PARENTS are like..."

Tamilda: "Heh... sure." (She doesn't. Not really.)


2. Maki turns over, head still in Tamilda's lap, and points at her new friend.

Maki: "I haven't seen you around. Did your parents drag you along 'cause they know the groom?"

Tamilda: "Actually, I'm here with Granny Dis--er, my granny. .. My parents are dead... I live on my own."


3. Maki jumps up and gets right in Tamilda's face. Tamilda blushes in surprise.

Maki: "What?? THAT'S SO COOL!"


4. Maki is embarrassed as she realizes what she just said. She grimaces.

Maki: "I, uh, meant the living on your own thing. Not the dead parents thing."

Tamilda: "Yeah."


5. Maki tries to dig herself out of this pit she's constructed. Tamilda finds it amusing.

Maki: "I mean, sometimes I wish MY parents were dead.  .. but, like, not REALLY. Y'know?"


6. Sweating but grinning, Maki tries to escape the situation with a distraction. She grabs Tamilda's hand and drags her away in a hurry.

Maki: "Hey, let's stop saying embarrassing things and go sneak some drinks from the bar!"

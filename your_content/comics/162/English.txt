1. The wedding is finally happening! Changeling-Kayra and Jonathan stand under an arch, before a cleric, holding hands and gazing into each other's eyes. The cleric - Naneek, an elf who was in Singing Cricket's adventuring party for a time - wears glasses and a mullet. The wedding party, from left to right: a burly dwarf woman with decorated beard and chainmail under her dress; a thin elf with an updo holding flowers; Kayra, still in human disguise, holding the bride's bouquet; Singing Cricket; Jonathan's younger brother, a young dark-skinned man with dreadlocks; and an older smiling gnome.

Someone is talking off-panel as the ceremony goes on (OK, that someone is Nanny Muk: "'scuse me. Coming through. [CRACK!] Whoops! Don't worry, dearie, that'll heal right up."


2. Nanny Muk (still in human disguise) sits down next to Granny Dismal (also in human disguise). A pointy eared woman with short hair glares at her for interrupting the ceremony.

Nanny Muk: "What'd I miss?"

Granny Dismal: "Most of the ceremony. Nothing important. Where is your halfling friend?"

Nanny Muk: "Recovering."


3. Changeling-Kayra and Jonathan grin at each other while the cleric performs a ritual, tying their entwined hands together with rope. Nanny Muk and Granny Dismal talk in captions.

Nanny Muk: "So she finally told him. And they're going through with it! You must be proud of yourself."

Granny Dismal: "I'm sure I don't know what you mean."


4. Kayra and Jonathan share their first kiss as wife and husband. Naneek (the cleric) smiles behind them. The hags' conversation continues in captions.

Nanny Muk: "Not a lot of folk willing to marry a changeling. You didn't influence him at all?"

Granny Dismal: "No. It appears to be 'True Love'."

Nanny Muk: "Amazing."


5. Changeling-Kayra swings Jonathan into a dip and kisses him passionately, to his surprise. Naneek looks annoyed. The hags' conversation continues.

Nanny Muk: "Looks like you've done well by her."

Granny Dismal: "I fulfilled the agreement."

Nanny Muk: "And then some! That's real happiness."


6. Jonathan and Kayra hold hands walking away down the aisle, which for some reason has a really nice rug on it (that's Hollering Elk's work). The rest of the wedding party follows behind. The other guests watch, smiling.

Nanny Muk: "Almost a shame what's going to happen later."

Granny Dismal: "We'll see."

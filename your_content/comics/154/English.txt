Changeling: "Mother, you've been fussing over me for hours." 

Mother: "It's just these few stubborn strands..."


2. Mother and daughter look at each other affectionately. 

Mother: "You long beautiful, dear. Even more than usual." 

Changeling: "Thank you." 

Mother: "And you've become such a wonderful, talented, lovely woman... You have always made me so proud."


3. Mother claps her hands. "now - *sniff* let's get you married!"

Changeling: "I know I breaks tradition, but I REALLY need to speak to Jonathan-" 

Mother: "Dear, Johnny loves you. There's nothing you can tell him that will change his mind."

Mother: "And the ceremony is about to start! You can talk so you like after the views are exchanged. It would be terribly bad luck if Johnny saw you in HERE - you'll see each other very soon!"

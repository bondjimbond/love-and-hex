1. Changeling Kayra and Jonathan walk out of the dressing room holding hands, to find original-Kayra (in human disguise) waiting for them. The changeling holds a bouquet of flowers.

Kayra: "Hi! Did you get everything off your chest?"

Changeling: "...Kayra? Is that you? I'm so glad you came!"

Changeling: "...Where's Sassy?"


2. In the background, Singing Cricket and Jonathan shake hands while the ladies talk.

Kayra: "Don't worry, she's... in GOOD HANDS."

Changeling-Kayra suppresses a giggle. "Oh! Well.. she deserves it. But that means I'm short one maid of honour...."


3. Changeling-Kayra leans in earnestly, surprising original-Kayra.

Changeling: "Kayra... would YOU stand up there with me?"

Kayra: "Me?? Are you sure?"

Changeling: "You're BASICALLY my SISTER. I'd have asked you in the first place if we'd known each other."

Kayra: "Then I'd love to!"


4. Kayra grins menacingly and slams her fist into her palm, surprising the changeling. Behind them, Jonathan asks about his halfling friend, worrying Singing Cricket.

Kayra: "So! Who do I need to fight?"

Changeling: "Huh?"


5. Kayra pulls a dagger out of her cleavage. The changeling grimaces awkwardly.

Kayra: "To defend your honour! His exes? YOUR exes? I've never used a battleaxe before... Is magic allowed? I have this dagger..."


6. Grinning worriedly, Changeling-Kayra passes her friend the bouquet.

Changeling: "Just.. hold these flowers."

Kayra, beaming: "Whatever you need!"
